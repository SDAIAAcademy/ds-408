# Analytics Products: Semantic Layers and Conversational Analytics

منتجات التحليلات: الطبقات الدلالية والتحليلات الحوارية

Data Science Track · SDAIA Academy · DS-408 · Level 400

Define metrics once as tested code, serve them through a governed semantic layer, and let an assistant answer only the questions it has proved it can answer

Participants build the analytics product of the fictional Qamar Telecom, whose 300 managers want to ask questions in Arabic and English about usage, billing, collections and complaints. They define metrics once as tested code with owners, design a semantic layer with conformed time, tariff history and access rules by region and classification, expose it to tools and agents, and evaluate a conversational assistant on certified questions, ending with a policy on which questions the assistant may answer. DS-307 covers single-area metrics, dashboards and a first agent test; this course designs the layer across business areas and governs the assistant.

**Format:** Intensive: 4 teaching days of four sessions and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

**Course page:** https://sdaiaacademy.github.io/ds-408/

## Case study

**Qamar Telecom** (قمر للاتصالات) is a fictional Saudi telecom operator. Its 300 managers want to ask, in Arabic and English, about usage, billing, collections and complaints, while finance, collections and customer care each report a different collection rate. Every lab works on its synthetic marts: about 2,400 customer accounts, 3,600 lines and 18 service months (January 2025 to June 2026) of usage, bills, payments and complaints, a tariff history with a price change, and a calendar with Gregorian, fiscal (April to March) and Hijri months. The data is generated by `labs/prepare_data.py` with a fixed seed, so every run gives the same numbers, and it carries planted problems that the labs find: a payment batch loaded twice, a fan-out between areas, a tariff join without validity dates, a dataset without an access rule, a field that copies a Restricted column without its label, and an expression that does not port to a second engine.

The assistant under test is **Daleel** (دليل). It turns a question into a query of the semantic layer. The labs run it with a scripted stand-in model that replays recorded, synthetic replies, so every participant gets the same scores; a free hosted model can be used instead (see `labs/README.md`).

People in the case: Jood Al-Muhaisen (finance controller), Majdi Bawazir (collections director), Ebtihal Al-Ghanim (head of customer care), Zeyad Al-Juraisy (data platform lead) and Eissa Al-Mahfouth (data governance and protection officer). The participant is the analytics product engineer.

## Capstone

A governed analytics product for two business areas (metrics as code, a semantic layer with tested access rules, an assistant evaluation on at least 40 certified questions, a routing policy) in a public repository whose README reports measured accuracy and stability, defended in a design review

**Submission:** a public GitHub repository link. The repository holds the metric files with their tests and the review rules in continuous integration, the semantic model with its access rules and access tests, the evaluation harness and its results on at least 40 certified questions (accuracy, wrong but plausible answers, stability over three runs), and the routing policy with its decision record. Its README reports the measured accuracy and stability. `labs/capstone/` holds a starter and a README template.

**Defence:** a 12-minute design review and 8 minutes of questions. During the questions the participant re-runs the evaluation on five questions the assessor adds.

**Rubric:** semantic model and metrics 30, access rules 15, assistant evaluation 30, routing policy 15, reproducibility 10.

## How the course is organised

- **Pre-work (guided self-study):** run `labs/00_setup_check.ipynb`, and revisit star schemas, fan-out and ratio metrics from DS-307 if they are not fresh.
- **Four teaching days** of four sessions. Each session is about 25 minutes of ideas and a 25-minute lab that starts from supplied files, so no lab depends on another.
- **Capstone day:** design reviews of the participants' products.
- **Follow-up task (tracked):** after the course, add one business area to the capstone product and re-run its evaluation; the trainer checks the repository's history.
- **Assessment:** a supervised check of modelling and metric fundamentals without AI assistance (20%), the capstone rubric (60%) and the live design review and defence (20%). Passing needs 70% overall and at least 60% on the capstone rubric.
- **AI policy:** AI-off for the supervised check and for Lab 1.1; an AI tutor in the other labs; full AI assistance in the capstone, coding agents included, with the design and evaluation defended live.
- **Responsible use:** access rules are tested, not assumed; Restricted customer fields are masked; the assistant shows the metric, filters and grain behind every figure; the routing policy is a human-oversight decision, recorded with its evidence.

## Prerequisites

- DS-307 Metrics, Semantic Models and Dashboards
- Writes SQL over a star schema, including window functions
- Has defined metrics in a semantic layer or a BI calculation language

## Tools

Each line is a capability; the examples are interchangeable.

- **Transformation framework:** SQLMesh, dbt
- **Semantic layer and metric definitions:** MetricFlow, Cube Core, Apache Ossie (Open Semantic Interchange) files
- **Analytical engine:** DuckDB, PostgreSQL
- **Access for tools and agents:** a Model Context Protocol server, a REST API over the semantic layer
- **Language model through a chat-completions style endpoint:** a locally served open-weight model, a free hosted model
- **Evaluation harness:** pytest with custom assertions, promptfoo, Inspect
