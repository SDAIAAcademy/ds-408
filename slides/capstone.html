Format

12 minutes of design review, then 8 minutes of questions with a live re-run

1 · The problem

Who, what, why now. One slide.

2 · What you built

Design, decisions, trade-offs. Then the live demo.

3 · What you measured

Accuracy, wrong but plausible, stability, the routes and their decision record.

The live re-run. In the questions the assessor gives five certified questions; add them and re-run your harness on screen.
Peer feedback. After each talk, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion, using the three lenses of Lab 4.4.

Keep time strictly: 12 minutes, then 8. The five assessor questions are prepared in advance from the participant's two areas (one Arabic, one must-refuse, one difficulty 3). The re-run counts toward reproducibility (10 points); a participant who cannot re-run explains why and shows the last results file with its input hashes.
